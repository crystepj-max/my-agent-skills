Combine two activities in the same session: use `grilling` to resolve material decisions, and use `domain-modeling` when terms or durable design decisions change. Follow those skills inline if they are not callable as separate tools. Keep unresolved decisions visible; do not invent a glossary or ADR when the conversation has not established one.
